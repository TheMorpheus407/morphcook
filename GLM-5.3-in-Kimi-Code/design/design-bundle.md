# Original Design Bundle

This folder mirrors the intent of the original Claude Design bundle that
seeded the product:

- `README.md` — the design language of record (palette, type, texture, voice)
- `tokens.md` — machine-readable design tokens
- `screen-shapes.md` — wireframe-level shapes of the key screens

The striped placeholders are **the** art direction for v1 — not a stand-in
for photography. Real photos are explicitly out of scope (see SPEC.md).
